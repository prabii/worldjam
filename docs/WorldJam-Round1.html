The problem

Music apps shut most people out

01

You need gear and training

Instruments, sample packs and theory come before the first beat. Most people never get there.

02

Built for eyes only

Timelines, piano rolls and tiny icons leave blind and low-vision users with little they can use on their own.

03

AI music lives in the cloud

Your room, your voice and your ideas get uploaded to a server, and nothing works offline.

The solution

Point at a cup. Record it. Play it.

Tap anything around you and WorldJam keeps its real sound. You program a beat with it, and on-device AI produces a full track around your beat.

Real sounds — your objects are the drums. No sample packs.

On-device AI — Gemma 4 plans the song, offline.

Audio-first — the camera names what it sees, out loud.

Choosing the object that matters

priority = 0.50 × confidence + 0.35 × size in frame + 0.15 × closeness to centre

ObjectConfidenceSizeCentrePriority
Laptop0.940.420.900.75 · primary
Bottle0.860.170.420.55
Chair0.780.240.300.52

The centre term is intent: you point the phone at what you want to know about.

Left, centre or right comes from where the object sits on screen. We never claim distance without a depth sensor.

Illustrative values, not measurements. All weights and thresholds are configurable and exposed in a developer diagnostics panel.
