The problem

Music apps shut most people out

01

You need gear and training

Instruments, sample packs and theory come before the first beat. Most people never get there.

02

Built for eyes only

Timelines, piano rolls and tiny icons leave blind and low-vision users with little they can use on their own.

03

AI music lives in the cloud

Your room, your voice and your ideas get uploaded to a server, and nothing works offline.

Three barriers: skill, sight, and the cloud. WorldJam removes all three at once.
