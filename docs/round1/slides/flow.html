How it works

Five steps from room to record

1

See

The camera spots objects and says the main one aloud.

NEW · in development

2

Capture

Hold, strike it, and its real sound is kept and named.

3

Beat

Tap an 8 or 16-step grid, or record the beat live.

4

Produce

Gemma keeps your beat and builds the rest of the track.

5

Share

Save the jam and export it as a WAV track.

Steps 2 to 5 run on the phone today. Step 1, See, is the vision and audio-readout layer being built in parallel on its own branch.
