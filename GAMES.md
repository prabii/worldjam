# WorldJam Games: How to Play

Both games use **the sounds you recorded yourself**. Capture a few objects first
(Home → **Scan**, or the **+** button), then open the **Play** tab.

---

## 🎹 Tiles

Tiles fall down three lanes in time with your beat. Tap each lane as its tile
reaches the bottom.

### Getting started
1. Open **Play → Tiles**.
2. Enter your name for the leaderboard, or tap **Skip**.
3. Choose what the tiles fall to:
   - **My beat grid**: the beat you programmed in Studio
   - **AI beat**: the pattern Gemma arranged (tap **Generate** if you have none)
   - **Saved jams**: any jam you've saved
   - With no beat at all, a default groove plays so you can still practise.
4. Tap **Start**.

### How to play
- There are three lanes: **left**, **centre** and **right**. Each lane is one of
  your recorded sounds, and its name is on the pad at the bottom.
- When a tile reaches the pad at the bottom of its lane, **tap anywhere in that
  lane**. The whole column counts, so you don't need to aim precisely.
- A hit plays that lane's sound. A tap with no tile nearby still plays the
  sound, but scores nothing.

### Scoring
| Timing | Grade | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Within 150 ms of the beat | **PERFECT** | 30 |
| Within 300 ms | **GOOD** | 20 |
| Up to 420 ms early or 320 ms late | **OK** | 10 |

- Points are **multiplied by your combo**: every hit in a row adds one to the
  multiplier.
- A missed tile resets the combo to zero.
- **Three misses and the game is over.**
- Your score, best combo and misses are saved to the leaderboard.

### Tips
- Listen as well as watch. The tiles land on the beat of your own music.
- Stay relaxed. A late tap still scores as OK; missing the tile completely
  breaks the combo.
- Put your darkest sound (a table or a book) in a steady lane. It usually
  carries the downbeat.

## 🔁 Echo

A memory game. The app plays a pattern of your sounds and you tap it back. The
pattern gets one hit longer every round.

### Getting started
1. You need **at least 2 captured sounds**. Echo uses up to 4 of them as pads.
2. Open **Play → Echo** and tap **Start**.

### How to play
1. **Listen.** The screen shows *"Listen…"* while the app plays the pattern.
   Each pad lights up as its sound plays.
2. **Your turn.** When it shows *"Your turn — 0/N"*, tap the pads in the same
   order.
3. **Get it right** and the pattern grows by one hit and plays again.
4. **Tap a wrong pad** and the round ends. The screen shows how long a pattern
   you reached.

### Scoring
- **Length** is how many hits are in the current pattern.
- **Best** is the longest pattern you've repeated correctly.
- Tap **Try again** to start a new pattern.

### Tips
- Remember the sounds, not just the positions. Hum the pattern back in your head.
- A pad never plays three times in a row, so if you've heard it twice, the next
  hit is somewhere else.
- The pads are your own objects. The better you know your kit, the easier it
  gets, and the faster you'll play in the Studio.
